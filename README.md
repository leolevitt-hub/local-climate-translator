# Local Climate Policy Impact

A web app that translates climate policy text into clear, locally relevant impacts and practical next steps based on a user’s situation (location, housing, transportation, work, and timing).

The goal is to help people understand **what a policy might realistically mean for them**, without jargon or inflated claims.

---

## What this tool does

- Takes short excerpts of climate or energy policy text
- Asks a few questions about the user’s situation
- Returns a structured, plain-English summary covering:
  - Who the policy is most relevant for
  - Likely impacts (housing, transportation, jobs, costs, etc.)
  - Actions someone could reasonably explore
  - What to check locally (utilities, landlords, state programs)
  - Uncertainties and open questions

This is an **interpretive aid**, not legal or financial advice.

---

## Tech stack

- **Next.js** (App Router)
- **React + TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **OpenAI API** (for policy analysis)
